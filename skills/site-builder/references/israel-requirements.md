# Israeli requirements (not legal advice)

Read from the sources in November 2026, summarized by site builders, not lawyers. Wording changes. Check the current text, and send the client to a lawyer for decisions.

## Accessibility
The Service Accessibility Regulations (2013), regulation 35, require adjustments in internet services under standard 5568, level AA. A statement placed prominently describes the adjustments, the accessibility coordinator if one is required, and contact details for reporting a problem. The copy we read includes exemptions by turnover and conditions, which need individual checking.
Sources disagree whether the standard is anchored in WCAG 2.0 or 2.1. Working target: 2.1 AA.
https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/accommodating_service_providing_rules/he/sitedocs_service_acessibility_regulations.pdf

## Privacy and cookies
Amendment 13 to the Privacy Protection Law took effect on 14.8.2025. The Privacy Protection Authority published a position on consent in February 2026. It covers what valid consent looks like, not when it is needed. Continued browsing can imply consent only where there is a reasonable link between the purpose and the service. Direct marketing unrelated to the transaction and profiling for it call for separate active consent. A text search of that paper did not find the word for cookies.
https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/consent-2026/he/cpncent-2025.pdf
Conservative default: non-essential analytics and marketing wait for consent.

## Marketing messages
Communications Law section 30A, 2008 text: email, SMS, fax and auto-dial advertising need prior explicit written consent, with a defined exception for a customer who gave details during a purchase or negotiation, under conditions. The word for "advertisement" opens the message and, in email, the subject line. Advertiser details and a simple way to refuse are required. A court may award damages without proof of harm, up to 1,000 NIS per message sent knowingly in breach.
https://fs.knesset.gov.il//17/law/17_lsr_299991.pdf
For a builder: a contact form is not newsletter consent. Use a separate unticked checkbox and keep proof.

## What the skill may say
It may report that a statement, policy, banner or checkbox exists, and compute contrast. It may draft text for review. It may never say the site complies.
