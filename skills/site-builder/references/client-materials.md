# What to ask the client for

Send as one list and track what arrived. Anything missing stays marked as missing.

- Logo files, brand colors and fonts if they exist
- Business details: legal name, phone, email, address, hours, social links
- Services or products, with real prices only if the client wants them shown
- Copy, or the facts needed to write it
- Testimonials with permission, numbers with a source, certificates
- Photos with usage rights
- Domain, hosting and DNS access, through the client's own accounts
- Who approves what, and who receives form leads
- Sites they like and dislike, and why
- Legal texts or their lawyer's contact
